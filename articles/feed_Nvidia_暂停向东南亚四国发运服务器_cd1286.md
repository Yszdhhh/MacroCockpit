FT just reported Nvidia froze south-east Asian server shipments days after Washington targeted Chinese labs renting cloud compute.

The pause follows a US Commerce Department interim rule requiring licences for advanced AI chips in those countries, plus Brazil.

Chinese AI labs have used the region to rent Nvidia compute from local cloud providers, sidestepping direct export bans.

Cloud providers now have 90 days to prove no restricted entities are among their users, yet nobody has explained how to apply.

The halt hits a big revenue line, since Nvidia booked $104B through its Singapore unit in 2025, up 72%.
