# Hackers breached the US Air Force's flagship Cloud One platform, report says

**Hackers breached the US Air Force's flagship cloud platform.** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/hackers-breach-us-air-force-flagship-cloud-one-computing-platform-2026-11-27/) reports that attackers penetrated Cloud One, the Air Force's central cloud infrastructure, potentially exposing sensitive tactical, personnel, and logistics data. The breach was discovered last month during routine system upgrades.

According to one source, the attackers exploited a software vulnerability to gain access to the platform's control plane. Cloud One has pooled cloud resources from Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle, and Alphabet since 2017, running applications that include air defense radar and munitions management. In response, the Air Force paused migration of additional applications, blocked updates, and revoked administrator privileges. US Cyber Command is assessing the damage.

The breach comes amid growing concern about AI-powered attacks, as AI models can rapidly identify vulnerabilities across large cloud networks. At least [six other cyber incidents have hit US government agencies](https://the-decoder.com/back-to-back-cyber-incidents-at-us-financial-investigators-and-spy-agencies-follow-a-string-of-federal-hacks-and-outages/) recently, and Microsoft's cybersecurity division reported widespread use of the AI tool [ARTEX](https://the-decoder.com/microsoft-details-how-state-backed-hackers-use-the-artex-ai-framework-to-automate-cyberattacks/). Who carried out the attack and whether any data was exfiltrated remain unknown. Ad
